# TamedMountables Plus

TamedMountables Plus is a community fork of VS_Horse, originally created by Ahrondus and the Feverstone Modding Team. It builds upon the original mod while introducing new mountable animals (camels), refining the riding experience, integrating TamedAI Plus commands, and adding quality-of-life improvements for a more immersive companion experience.

A huge thank you to Ahrondus, Verlia, and the Feverstone Modding Team for creating and maintaining VS_Horse, one of the most beloved mount and animal mods in the Vintage Story community.

## What's New

### New Mountable Animals

* **Camels** — A fully rideable desert mount with wild and tamed variants, complete with their own gaits, idle animations, and AI behaviors.
* Both camels and horses can be ridden, equipped with saddles, bridles, and saddlebags.

### TamedAI Plus Integration

* Full compatibility with TamedAI Plus, including:
  * **Follow Master** — Your mount will follow you when commanded.
  * **Stay** — Your mount will wait at a chosen location.
  * **Roam** — Your mount can wander freely.
  * **Guard** — Your mount will patrol and defend an area.
  * **Aggression Levels** — Control how your mount reacts to threats (Neutral, Protective, Aggressive, Passive).
  * **Pet Profile** — View species, gender, generation, and obedience.

### Improved Riding & Gaits

* Multiple gaits for realistic movement (walk, trot, canter, gallop).
* Seamless gait transitions when riding.
* Horse breeds with unique stats (Percheron, Arabian, Shire, Quarter Horse, Donkey).

### Enhanced Equipment

* Breed-specific saddles, bridles, saddlebags, and pillion cushions.
* Equipment can be crafted and equipped for both horses and camels.

### Fixed & Refined Behaviors

* Fixed camel rideable initialization crash when placing tamed variants.
* Rebalanced AI task priorities so animals move and wander naturally.
* Added proper walking animations for tamed camels.
* Fixed camel shape animation frame count (off-by-one error).

## Disclaimer

* AI was used to assist in the development and improvement of this mod.
* Translations other than English have not been verified.
* This mod is designed to be used alongside TamedAI Plus.
* Compatibility with all third-party mount and animal mods has not yet been fully tested.
